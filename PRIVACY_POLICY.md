# IndiaAssist AI — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

IndiaAssist AI ("the app") helps people understand Indian government services. It is **not** an official government app and is not affiliated with any government body.

## What we collect
- **Account:** your email address and a password (handled by Firebase Authentication).
- **Profile (optional):** name, age, state, occupation, whether you are a senior citizen.
- **Reminders:** the title, due date and note you enter.
- **Document names:** the names/types of documents you add to the Document Locker.

## What stays on your phone
The document files you add to the Document Locker are stored **only on your device**. They are not uploaded to our servers. They are lost if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## AI assistant
Questions you type in the assistant are sent to a third-party AI provider (Groq) to generate a reply. Please do not enter Aadhaar/PAN numbers, OTPs, passwords or bank details in chats.

## Location
If you allow it, your location is used once to detect your state so the app can show state-specific information. It is not stored.

## How we use data
Only to run the app: login, show your profile and reminders, and answer your questions. We do not sell your data and show no ads.

## Who processes data
Google Firebase (authentication and database) and Groq (AI replies).

## Your rights and deleting your data
You can delete your account and all associated data inside the app: **Profile → ⋮ menu → Delete Account**. To ask a question or make a request, contact **[YOUR EMAIL]**.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.

## Disclaimer
Information in the app is gathered from official sources but fees, rules and portals change. Always confirm on the official website (.gov.in / .nic.in) before applying or paying.
